# Privacy

The published version of this policy, in Korean and English, is the page in `web/privacy/`.

Sentinel Lab does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- The app makes no connection to the internet. It has no account, analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
- Messages from your boards are processed in memory on your PC. They are written to disk only when you export a log or a report, to a location you choose.
- The broker password you set is stored on your PC, encrypted with the operating system's credential protection.
- Layout, rules and progress are stored locally in the app's user data folder. Removing the app's data removes them.
- Links to firmware documentation open in your own browser; those sites have their own policies.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
